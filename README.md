## 🌙 About

**AUROLYNE** is a personal digital world designed to bring together journaling, dreams, ideas, projects, and small digital companions in one magical space.

Instead of being just another notes or productivity app, AUROLYNE focuses on creating a more **personal, immersive, and expressive experience**.

It is a place where your thoughts can become memories, your dreams can become stories, and your ideas can slowly grow into something more.

---

## ✦ Concept

AUROLYNE combines elements of:

* 📖 **Personal journaling**
* 🌙 **Dream documentation**
* 💡 **Ideas & projects**
* 🐾 **Digital companions**
* 🎨 **Personal expression**
* ✨ **AI-powered interaction**
* 🌌 **A small persistent digital world**

The goal is simple:

> **Create a digital space that feels like it belongs to you.**

---

## 🪄 Features

### 📖 Personal Grimoire

Keep thoughts, experiences, dreams, ideas, and personal entries organized in your own digital space.

### 🌙 Dream Journal

Record and preserve dreams before they disappear into the night.

### 💡 Ideas & Projects

Capture creative ideas and gradually turn them into projects.

### 🐾 Digital Companions

Take care of small digital creatures that can interact and grow alongside you.

### 🤖 AI Interaction

AI-assisted feedback and interaction designed to make the experience feel more alive and personal.

### 🎨 Pixel World

A stylized pixel-art environment where your avatar and companions can exist beyond traditional app interfaces.

## 📐 Project Scope

AUROLYNE is intentionally designed as a manageable academic and personal project.

The first version focuses on delivering the core experience without requiring:

* Large-scale infrastructure
* Expensive cloud services
* Massive databases
* A public social network
* A full game engine

The architecture should remain flexible enough to grow if the project evolves in the future.
